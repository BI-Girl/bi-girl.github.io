Retail Analytics Dashboard

Home Projects

📌 Business Problem

The business needed a centralized dashboard to monitor sales performance, margins, store KPIs and discount impact across regions.

📊 Solution

Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with time intelligence, drill-through analysis, KPI tracking and store-level insights.

🧠 Key Insights

  • Identified high-performing and underperforming stores
  • Highlighted margin erosion due to excessive discounting
  • Improved decision-making with real-time KPIs

🛠 Tools Used

Power BI, DAX, SQL, Excel
